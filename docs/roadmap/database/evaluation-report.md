# Phase 3 — Evaluation & Curation Report

## Purpose

This report evaluates all 19 curated resources in the EnglishOS database against pedagogical quality, hidden-gem value, EnglishOS fit, roadmap fit, and evidence strength.

Each resource receives a scorecard. Resources are classified as **Approved**, **Approved (Featured)**, or **Rejected**.

---

## Scoring Rubric

| Score | Pedagogical | Hidden Gem | EnglishOS Fit | Roadmap Fit | Confidence |
|-------|-------------|------------|---------------|-------------|------------|
| 9-10 | Exceptional. Best in class. | Unknown even to experienced learners. | Perfect fit. Core to mission. | Fits multiple blocks. | Very high. Multiple strong sources. |
| 7-8 | Strong. Well-designed. | Underrecognised. | Strong fit. Clear value. | Fits 1-2 blocks. | High. Consistent positive evidence. |
| 5-6 | Solid. Does the job. | Moderately known. | Good fit. Some limitations. | Fits one block. | Moderate. Some evidence. |
| 3-4 | Adequate. Basic. | Well-known. | Marginal fit. | Weak fit. | Low. Anecdotal only. |
| 1-2 | Weak. Poor design. | Mainstream/hype. | Poor fit. | No clear block. | Very low. No evidence. |
